## 项目自有内容 / Project-owned material

根目录 [LICENSE](LICENSE) 的 MIT 许可证适用于项目有权许可的代码、技能指令、测试、文档、原创词条解释与例句、索引编排，以及 `assets/cover.webp` 和 `assets/icon.png`。再分发相应内容时须保留版权与许可文本；视觉素材中出现的独立第三方名称或标识仍适用其自身权利。

The root [LICENSE](LICENSE) applies to project-controlled code, skill instructions, tests, documentation, original entry explanations and examples, catalogue arrangement, and `assets/cover.webp` and `assets/icon.png`. Redistribution must retain the copyright and permission notice; independent third-party names or marks shown in visual material retain their own rights.

项目内视觉资源和外部来源的逐项登记见 [`references/rights.json`](references/rights.json)。该登记保存项目资源的当前字节摘要、用途与项目许可范围，并为每个 `source_id` 明确记录：项目只保存来源元数据和用于支持有限命题的项目自有表述，不打包或再许可链接目标的完整正文、音视频或图片。

The object-level registry is [`references/rights.json`](references/rights.json). It records current project-asset byte identities, purpose, and project-license scope, and states for every external `source_id` that the repository keeps source metadata and project-authored claim support rather than bundling or relicensing the linked full article, video, audio, or image.

## 外部来源与作品 / External sources and works

- `references/sources.json` 中的标题、发布方、日期和 URL 用于定位外部资料。项目许可不覆盖链接的新闻、视频、弹幕、配音、音乐、歌词、图片、人物肖像或第三方创作。
- 梗名、短台词和作品名用于识别、说明与索引；项目许可不转授这些外部表达、人物形象、商标或音视频素材本身的权利。
- “哔哩哔哩”及相关标识的商标权不由本项目许可。本项目描述 B站文化，不表示与平台官方存在关联。

Source titles, publishers, dates, and URLs identify external material. The project license does not cover linked articles, videos, comments, dubbing, music, lyrics, images, portraits, third-party creations, characters, or trademarks. Bilibili-related names identify the subject matter and do not imply official affiliation.

## 收录范围 / Inclusion boundary

词条只保存必要事实、独立撰写的解释、原创短例句、来源元数据和内容页链接。新增资料应记录发布方、核查日期、实际读取范围、支持命题和具体局限。没有适用许可或法律依据时，不收录原视频、长段台词、完整歌词、截图、表情包或第三方图片。

Entries contain necessary facts, independently written explanations, short original examples, source metadata, and links. New material should record its publisher, check date, access depth, supported claims, and limitations. Do not bundle videos, long transcripts, full lyrics, screenshots, reaction images, or third-party artwork without an applicable permission or legal basis.
